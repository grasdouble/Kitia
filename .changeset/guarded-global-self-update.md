Update Kitia in place when installed globally with npm or pnpm
  - **User-Facing**
    - Run `kitia self-update` or choose **Update** in the dashboard's new-version notice to update the current Kitia installation after confirmation.
    - Leave local dependencies, one-off launcher caches, and installations of unknown origin untouched.
